# Desktop, Portable, a manual install or Cloud

<!-- lesson: choose-setup -->
Lesson "choose-setup", in the group **Start and a reliable setup** of the lesson list. Level 0, core; track Base. It
follows the new standard of lessons (standard 2) and comes before [How ComfyUI works](How-ComfyUI-Works.md).

## What you will be able to do
Choose a way to run ComfyUI that fits your computer, your privacy, the packs you need and your budget, and name its
limits, before you install anything.

## What you need
- Nothing installed yet. You need to know your operating system and your graphics card, or where to look them up.
- Background: [ComfyUI and where to get it](../start/ComfyUI.md).
- Written against ComfyUI 0.45.0 and the official documentation (system requirements, Comfy Cloud), checked on
  2026-11-15.

## The steps
1. **Four ways to run the same ComfyUI** (reading).
2. **Your facts first** (checklist, your own word): system, graphics card and its memory, free disk, privacy, packs,
   money.
3. **The three local ways** (reading): Comfy Desktop (Windows, macOS on Apple chips, Linux; follows the stable
   release; snapshots before updates), Portable (Windows, NVIDIA or the processor alone), a manual install (any system
   and graphics card; comfy-cli does the same steps).
4. **Comfy Cloud** (reading): no install, no graphics card; a fixed set of packs you cannot add to, online only, a free
   tier and paid plans, a time limit per run.
5. **Write your choice**: a Text (Multiline) node on the canvas with the way, two limits and what would change it.
6. **Is the choice sound?** (rubric): it runs on your system, two limits named, nothing installed "to try".
7. **Check yourself** (three questions).
8. **On your own: someone else's computer** (checklist, no **Do this for me**).

## What the lesson checks
- On the canvas, by the app: the card is a Text (Multiline) node with a text of a few lines.
- Not checked: your computer, your facts, whether the choice is right. The end of the lesson says **Result assessed:
  your own word**.

## Mistakes people make
- Two or three installs "to try", with models in several folders: keep one, or share one model folder.
- Cloud chosen, then a workflow needs a pack Cloud does not have: Cloud runs a fixed set of packs.
- Portable on a Mac or Linux: Portable is for Windows.
- A choice made from a video's "best", not from your own facts.
