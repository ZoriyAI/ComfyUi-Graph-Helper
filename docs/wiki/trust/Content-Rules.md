# Content rules

CozyGraph teaches how to build graphs in your own ComfyUI. The subject of your work is your choice and your
responsibility. These rules say what the lessons cover and what to keep out of your work; the app and its lessons
state them in the same words. The assistant is a model you choose: the app adds no subject rules of its own to it,
and each provider or local model answers by its own rules.

## The rules

Adults (18+) may make any lawful adult content with the consent of everyone involved, and the app explains the technique. Keep these out of your work:
- minors and anyone who looks like a minor, drawn characters included;
- real people in explicit content; use a person's face or voice only with their consent;
- sexual violence and coercion;
- animals in explicit content;
- anything the law forbids where you live.

What you make and where you post it is your responsibility: the laws of your country and the rules of the sites.

## The switch "Adult content (18+)"

It is in Settings ▸ Privacy and is off in a new profile. Turning it on shows these rules and asks you to confirm with
"I am 18 or older". The app never asks for your age in any other way.

While it is on:

- the previews and example descriptions that the author of a model marked as adult content are shown in the model
  card and in the list of models;
- the lessons for adults are open.

While it is off:

- what an author marked as adult content stays closed. A model card says that it is closed and can show it for that
  one file when you ask;
- the lessons for adults stay in the list as one closed row, "For adults (18+)", with a button that leads to the
  switch.

Turning it off takes effect at once and asks nothing.

## How the lessons for adults teach

- They contain no explicit texts: no ready descriptions of scenes and no lists of such words. They teach technique:
  which kinds of models and LoRAs exist, how a model reads a description, where the author of a model writes how to
  use it, LoRA strength, settings and how to fix defects. You write your own words.
- The app never downloads, installs or runs anything. It reads what is already in your ComfyUI and helps you build a
  graph; you run the graph in ComfyUI yourself.
- What the app shows of a model is what its author wrote next to the file or on the model's page. The app does not
  check pictures; it relies on the mark of the author or of the site that hosts the model.

## Reporting a problem with content

- Something that can be discussed in public, such as a text in the app or in this documentation: open an Issue with
  the label `content-rules`.
- Something that should not be public: use GitHub's private reporting (the repository's Security tab, Report a
  vulnerability).

Please describe what you saw and where in the app.
