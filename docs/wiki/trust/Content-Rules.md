# Subject choice and adult education

The app is a general technical tutor for graphs, subgraphs, nodes, models, LoRAs, helpers and prompts. The subject is your choice and responsibility. The app does not add subject-based prohibitions to the assistant system prompt. Your selected provider or local model still responds according to its own rules and capabilities.

Adult teaching is factual and non-graphic, about lawful consensual 18+ use. Use adult subjects, exclude minors and anyone who appears underage, obtain consent for a real person's likeness or voice, and permission for source material. Check the laws that apply to you and the rules of the places where you share results. These are educational guidelines, not a claim that the app verifies ages, consent or legality.

## Previews and examples

Settings ▸ Privacy controls whether previews and examples marked as adult content are shown. With the preference off, you can reveal them for an individual model card and hide them again. This visibility setting is your choice; it is not a topic filter for technical questions. No generation runs here: the app reads ComfyUI and exports workflows for you to run there.
