# Settings: Appearance and animation

**Appearance & Motion**: "Language, motion, density and the action colour. Language switches at once; the rest applies after Save."

| Field | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Motion** | **System** | **System** (follows the setting of your computer), **On**, **Reduced**, **Off**. "The choice applies after Save, not while drafting." |
| **Language** | the language of your system at the first start | **English** or **Русский**. "The window switches immediately, and Save stores the choice." |
| **Density** | **Comfortable** | **Comfortable** or **Compact**: how much room the controls take. |
| **Accent colour** | **Cyan** | **Cyan**, **Violet**, **Mint**, **Amber**: the colour of the main button and of what is selected. |
| **Reduce glow** | off | "Flatter buttons and canvas without soft light." |

**Preview** at the bottom shows a sample node ("Sampler", "Turns noise into an image in steps.") and a **Primary action** button with your choices.

Language and motion are also in the **View** menu, where they are saved at once.
