# Settings: Appearance and animation

**Appearance & Motion**: "Language, motion, density and the action color. Language switches at once; the rest applies after Save."

| Field | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Motion** | **System** | **System** (follows the setting of your computer), **On**, **Reduced**, **Off**. "System, On, Reduced or Off. The choice applies after “Save settings”." See below. |
| **Language** | the language of your system at the first start: Russian on a Russian system, otherwise English | **English** or **Русский**. "English or Русский. The window switches at once, and “Save settings” keeps the choice." The welcome card has a language button too. |
| **Density** | **Comfortable** | **Comfortable** or **Compact**: how much room the controls take. |
| **Interface size** | 100 % | From 80 to 200 %. "Makes every text, button and panel larger or smaller. The canvas zoom stays its own." The View menu has the same: `Ctrl+Alt+=` larger, `Ctrl+Alt+-` smaller, `Ctrl+Alt+0` normal. |
| **Accent color** | **Cyan** | **Cyan**, **Violet**, **Mint**, **Amber**: the colour of the main button and of what is selected. |
| **Reduce glow** | off | "Flatter buttons and canvas without soft light." |

**Preview** at the bottom shows a sample node ("Sampler", "Turns noise into an image in steps.") and a **Primary action** button with your choices.

Language and motion are also in the **View** menu, where they are saved at once.

## Motion

**Motion** applies to everything that moves in the window.

- **On**: things travel and loop. The view glides to where it goes; a short hop takes about a quarter of a second, a long trip about two thirds.
- **Reduced**, and **System** when your system asks to reduce motion: only short fades. Nothing loops or travels, and the view jumps.
- **Off**: nothing animates. Marks still appear and then go.

What moves, with motion on: the place of a lesson step stands out and the rest dims; what passed a step flashes; during a tour of the graph the step's node and its feeders stand out; after a check a wave runs over the marked nodes; a grey draft turns into the node when accepted and dissolves when rejected; a dashed line shows the best input while a link is dragged; a history replay shows what each version changed; a deleted node fades where it stood; chat tool cards that arrive together come in one after another. See [The canvas](Canvas.md#what-moves-on-the-canvas).

## Limits

There is one dark theme. No light theme.
