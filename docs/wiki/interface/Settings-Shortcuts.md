# Settings: Shortcuts

"Choose Change, then press the new keys. Esc cancels. Shortcuts apply after “Save settings”." The section lists every command of the app, by menu (File, Edit, View, Graph, Assistant, Sync, Help), with its shortcut.

## How to change a shortcut

1. Find the command in the list. Rows such as "Language: English", "Choose the assistant: OpenAI" or "Color: Red" are the choices of a submenu.
2. Press **Change**. The row says "Press keys…".
3. Press the new combination, for example `Ctrl+Shift+K`. `Escape` cancels, and `Tab` leaves the row. A key without `Ctrl` or `Alt` that moves around the window or types text is refused: "This key moves around the window or types text. Hold Ctrl or Alt with it. Esc cancels."
4. If the combination already belongs to another command: "Ctrl+K already belongs to “Add node…”. Move it to “…”? “Add node…” is then left without a shortcut." **Move it** or **Keep as it was**.
5. Press **Save settings**.

## Other buttons on a row

- the cross (**Remove**): the command gets no shortcut ("None");
- the round arrow (**Reset**): back to the default; "Default: Ctrl+K" shows it.

**Reset all to defaults** at the top resets every row. A conflict between two rows is marked "Also used by …".

## Good to know

- `Ctrl` is the key on Linux; on a Mac it is `Cmd`.
- On a Latin keyboard (QWERTY, AZERTY, QWERTZ, Dvorak) a shortcut is the key marked with its letter. On a Russian or another non-Latin layout it is the key in the same place as on a US keyboard. Where that matters, a row says so: "On this keyboard: the Я key", or "On this keyboard: type it with Shift or AltGr".
- `Alt` and `F10` (menu bar) and `Shift+F10` (canvas menu) cannot be changed.
