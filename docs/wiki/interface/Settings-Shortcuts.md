# Settings: Shortcuts

"Choose Change, then press the new keys. Escape cancels. Shortcuts apply after Save." The section lists every command of the app, by menu (File, Edit, View, Graph, Assistant, Sync, Help), with its shortcut.

## How to change a shortcut

1. Find the command in the list. Rows such as "Language: English" or "Who answers: OpenAI" are the choices of a submenu.
2. Press **Change**. The row says "Press keys…".
3. Press the new combination, for example `Ctrl+Shift+K`. `Escape` cancels.
4. If the combination already belongs to another command: "Ctrl+K already belongs to “Add node…”. Move it to “…”? “Add node…” is then left without a shortcut." **Move it** or **Keep as it was**.
5. Press **Save settings**.

## Other buttons on a row

- the cross (**Remove**): the command gets no shortcut ("None");
- the round arrow (**Reset**): back to the default; "Default: Ctrl+K" shows it.

**Reset all to defaults** at the top resets every row. A conflict between two rows is marked "Also used by …".

## Good to know

- `Ctrl` is the key on Linux; letters match by the physical key, so a Russian layout works.
- `Alt` and `F10` (menu bar) and `Shift+F10` (canvas menu) cannot be changed.
