# Settings

Settings is a window over the app with eight sections on the left: **Assistant**, **Connection**, **Sync**, **Canvas**, **Appearance & Motion**, **Shortcuts**, **Privacy** and **About**. "Changes take effect when you press “Save settings”."

<!-- picture: the Settings window on the Assistant section -->

## How to open it

- The gear at the right end of the toolbar.
- **File ▸ Settings…** or `Ctrl+,`.
- **Assistant ▸ Assistant settings…** opens it on Assistant; **Sync ▸ Sync settings…** on Sync.
- Buttons in messages, such as **Open connection settings**, open it on the right section.

## How saving works

1. Change what you need, in one section or in several. Nothing applies yet: the window holds a draft.
2. Press **Save settings** at the bottom. The line at the bottom left says "Settings saved."
3. **Cancel**, `Escape` or a click outside closes the window. If you changed something, the app asks: "Close without saving? The changes made in Settings since the last save will be lost." **Close without saving** or **Keep editing**.

Some things apply at once, without Save:

- the language (the window switches at once; Save remembers it);
- automatic sync and its interval in the sync popover;
- a key or token you paste: it is checked and used at once;
- **Forget all keys and tokens** and **Clear chat history**.

## The line at the bottom

"Settings loaded.", "Default settings, not saved yet.", "Settings are not saved yet.", "Nothing to save: nothing has changed yet.", "Fix the highlighted fields first." (a field is marked red, for example "The MCP server address is not an address yet. Fix it in Connection; the field is marked."), "Settings could not be saved.", "Stored settings could not be read; default settings are shown."

## Rules for every field

- A field cannot hold a key or a password: "Settings cannot hold a key or a password. Remove it from the field." Keys go into their own key fields, which keep them outside the settings file.
- The window can be made larger by its bottom right corner.
