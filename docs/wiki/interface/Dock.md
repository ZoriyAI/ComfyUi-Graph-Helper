# The right panel

The right panel holds four tabs: **Assistant**, **Explain**, **Inspector** and **Check**. Each tab keeps its state when you switch away: a message you started to write, the scroll position, a field you were editing.

<!-- picture: the right panel with its four tab icons and the two buttons on the right -->

## The tabs

| Tab | Icon | What it is for | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Assistant** | sparkles | the conversation with the assistant, the modes Ask, Build and Learn, the lessons | [The assistant tab](Assistant-Panel.md), [The lessons tab](Lessons-Panel.md) |
| **Explain** | light bulb | the explanations of what is selected, and your own notes | [The explanations tab](Explain-Panel.md) |
| **Inspector** | sliders | the selected node, link or the whole graph | [Properties](Properties.md) |
| **Check** | list with ticks | the findings of the check | [The check tab](Check-Panel.md) |

When the names do not fit, the tabs show only their icons; hover to see the name.

## How to show, hide and size it

- **Hide tools** (the right icon in the header), **View ▸ Tools panel** or `Ctrl+J` hides the panel. A strip of icons stays on the right edge: **Show tools** and one icon per tab.
- Drag the left edge (**Resize tools**) to make it from 320 to 720 pixels wide. The canvas keeps at least 560 pixels.
- **Show both** (the left icon in the header, or **View ▸ Show both**) shows Inspector and Assistant side by side. The header then names both; a click on a name goes back to one tab. It needs a window at least 1280 pixels wide; when the room is short, the library folds by itself, and when it is still short the button is grey and says why.

## What opens a tab by itself

- **Check graph** opens **Check**.
- **Message the assistant**, a question from a canvas menu, and **Lessons: build it yourself** open **Assistant**.
- **Rename…** and **Note for this node…** open **Inspector**.
