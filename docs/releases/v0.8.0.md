# Graph Helper for ComfyUI 0.8.0

Five new lessons on the WanVideoWrapper node pack, and File ▸ New project now asks before it clears a graph you
never saved.

## New lessons

- **Animation by pose: UniAnimate.** The person in a picture repeats the movements of a person in a video. You need
  a Wan 2.1 "picture to video" model and one LoRA. A node of the pack itself draws the skeleton.
- **Two people talking: InfiniteTalk.** Follows the lesson "A talking video". A second voice recording, the order
  of the lines, and who speaks with which voice: the first voice goes to the person on the left, the second to the
  person on the right.
- **A talking portrait: FantasyTalking.** A short voice recording and a portrait. The graph is simpler than with
  InfiniteTalk. The lesson checks that the sound is as long as the clip.
- **A video with sound: Ovi.** One run draws the clip and its sound. What the person in the video says goes into the
  description between the marks `<S>` and `<E>`. The lesson checks that the marks are there.
- **Another camera: ReCamMaster.** A finished video is redrawn with another camera movement: pan, tilt, push in,
  orbit. The lesson describes ten movements.
- Lessons that give a person a face or a voice first talk about that person's consent.
- The nodes, inputs and outputs of these lessons were checked against the source of WanVideoWrapper 1.4.8.

## Good to know

- The speech recognition node in the FantasyTalking lesson and the pose detection node in the UniAnimate lesson
  download their own small models the first time the graph runs in ComfyUI. The lessons say so. The app itself
  still downloads nothing into ComfyUI.

## Fixes

- File ▸ New project asks before it clears the canvas also when the graph came from the previous start of the app
  and was never saved to a file. Before, such a graph was cleared without asking (it stayed in the history of
  versions, but you might not know that).
- The class name under a node's title shows in full in a tooltip.

## Installing

`sudo apt install ./comfyui-graph-builder_*.deb`. Settings, keys and projects are kept.
